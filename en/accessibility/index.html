Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Ailin Amondaray is committed to making the website turdustattoo.com accessible, taking as a reference the UNE-EN 301549:2022 standard and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Compliance Status

The website turdustattoo.com has been reviewed and meets the Level AA accessibility requirements of WCAG 2.1 and the applicable requirements of the UNE-EN 301549:2022 standard.

  • Accessibility level: AA (WCAG 2.1).
  • Date of the most recent review: 07/09/2026.

Reporting Accessibility Issues and Suggestions

Anyone who experiences difficulties accessing the content or features of this website may report accessibility issues, submit suggestions for improvement or request information about its accessibility.
